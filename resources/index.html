✍️ Consent Checklists — Risks & Benefits

Standardised summaries for commonly performed procedures. Designed for quick reference during consent conversations — not exhaustive, but covering the key points discussed in GMC-compliant consent. Always tailor to the individual patient.

HPB / General Surgery

Laparoscopic Cholecystectomy

  • Benefits: Relief of biliary symptoms; prevention of recurrent cholecystitis, pancreatitis, or biliary colic
  • Common (1–10%): Wound infection, port-site hernia, diarrhoea (post-cholecystectomy syndrome), minor bile leak
  • Infrequent (<1%): Bile duct injury, conversion to open, retained CBD stone, major haemorrhage, bowel injury, DVT/PE
  • Very rare: Mortality (~0.1–0.3%), bile duct stricture, hepatic artery injury
HPB Surgery

Pancreaticoduodenectomy (Whipple)

  • Benefits: Curative resection for pancreatic/periampullary malignancy; symptom palliation
  • Common (10–40%): Delayed gastric emptying, pancreatic fistula (POPF), wound infection
  • Infrequent (1–10%): Post-pancreatectomy haemorrhage, bile leak, intra-abdominal abscess, DVT/PE, pneumonia, new-onset diabetes
  • Rare (<1–3%): Mortality (1–3%), myocardial infarction, CVA, multi-organ failure
HPB Surgery

Distal Pancreatectomy & Splenectomy

  • Benefits: Resection of pancreatic body/tail lesions; curative intent for malignancy
  • Common (10–30%): Pancreatic fistula (POPF), splenectomy-related (thrombocytosis, OPSI risk)
  • Infrequent (1–10%): Post-pancreatectomy haemorrhage, intra-abdominal collection/abscess, wound infection, new/worsened diabetes
  • Rare (<1%): Mortality (<1%), gastric/colonic ischaemia, portal vein thrombosis
HPB Surgery

Liver Resection

  • Benefits: Curative resection for primary or metastatic liver tumours; symptom control for large benign lesions
  • Common (5–20%): Bile leak, wound infection, pleural effusion, transient liver dysfunction
  • Infrequent (1–5%): Post-hepatectomy liver failure, haemorrhage, intra-abdominal collection, DVT/PE, incisional hernia
  • Rare (<1–3%): Mortality (1–3%), biliary stricture, hepatic artery thrombosis
HPB / Gastroenterology

ERCP ± Sphincterotomy

  • Benefits: Clearance of CBD stones; biliary drainage for obstruction/stricture; diagnostic tissue sampling
  • Common (5–10%): Post-ERCP pancreatitis, transient rise in amylase/lipase
  • Infrequent (1–5%): Haemorrhage (post-sphincterotomy), cholangitis, duodenal perforation, stent migration
  • Rare (<1%): Mortality (~0.1–0.5%), severe necrotising pancreatitis, retroperitoneal perforation
General Surgery

Inguinal Hernia Repair (Open / Laparoscopic)

  • Benefits: Relief of symptoms; prevention of incarceration/strangulation; mesh reinforcement
  • Common (1–10%): Seroma/haematoma, wound infection, chronic groin pain (10–12%), urinary retention
  • Infrequent (<1–2%): Recurrence, testicular atrophy/swelling, ilioinguinal nerve injury, mesh infection
  • Very rare: Bowel/bladder injury, major vascular injury (laparoscopic), mortality
General Surgery

Laparoscopic Appendicectomy

  • Benefits: Definitive treatment of appendicitis; prevention of perforation and peritonitis
  • Common (1–10%): Wound infection (especially if perforated), intra-abdominal collection, port-site hernia
  • Infrequent (<1–2%): Conversion to open, stump leak, bowel injury, negative appendicectomy
  • Very rare: Mortality (<0.1%), delayed recognition of alternative pathology
Colorectal Surgery

Right Hemicolectomy

  • Benefits: Curative resection for right-sided colonic malignancy or large polyps not amenable to endoscopic resection
  • Common (5–15%): Wound infection, ileus, incisional hernia
  • Infrequent (1–5%): Anastomotic leak, intra-abdominal collection, DVT/PE, cardiac/respiratory complications
  • Rare (<1–2%): Mortality (elective <2%), ureteric injury, duodenal injury, CVA

📚 Online Atlases & Anatomy References

High-quality online resources for surgical anatomy, radiological correlation, and operative planning.

  • 🎬 Toronto Video Atlas of Surgery (TVASurg) Open-access library of animation-enhanced surgical videos curated by surgeons from world-class institutions. 3D reconstructions with real-time operative footage — excellent for understanding complex HPB and transplant procedures.
  • 🧠 TeachMeAnatomy Free, comprehensive anatomy resource with excellent surgical relevance. Concise articles, diagrams, and clinical correlations. Ideal for FRCS revision.
  • 🩻 Radiopaedia Collaborative radiology wiki with thousands of annotated cases. Essential for correlating surgical anatomy with cross-sectional imaging.
  • 🎥 Acland's Video Atlas of Human Anatomy Rotating cadaveric dissection videos showing real anatomical relationships in three dimensions. Subscription required but widely available via NHS OpenAthens.
  • 🖥️ e-Anatomy (IMAIOS) High-resolution cross-sectional atlas with CT/MRI labelling. Excellent for pre-operative planning and understanding segmental anatomy.
  • 🏛️ RCS England Library & e-Resources Access to surgical journals, e-books, and databases including UpToDate and ClinicalKey via RCS membership.
  • 📱 Touch Surgery Interactive surgical simulation app. Step-by-step walkthroughs of common procedures. Useful for trainees preparing for cases.
  • 📖 Surgical Journals — Key HPB Publications HPB (Oxford), Annals of Surgery, BJS, JAMA Surgery, Surgery, JACS, Annals of Surgical Oncology.
